# Credit-Analysis-on-Green-Solutions-Manufacturing-Ldt

A credit assessment was done on a company, Green Solutions Manufacturing Ltd. Green Solutions is a manufacturing company that specialises in manufacturing sustainable drinkware (including reusable water bottles and coffee cups) with a particular focus on sustainable practices.

The credit analysis was done quantitatively and qualitatively to determine if they qualified for credit.

Quantitative Analysis: Entailed analysing the company's financial statements and financial ratios to determine if they were in a healthy financial position to be able to repay the loan.

Qualitative Analysis: Involved conducting market research on the market the business is operating in to determine macroeconomic factors that could affect the business and explore growth prospects of the business in the market.

At the end of the report, after having done a thorough credit analysis, it was determined that Green Solutions was a low risk client and could be offered the business loan to expand their operations.
